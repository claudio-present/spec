# Traceability

Permanent, immutable archive of `Closed` [Open Questions](./open-questions.md): the full discussion, alternatives considered, and the decision that resolved each one. Keeps `open-questions.md` and `requirements/` clean — only the final requirement (in EARS) and a one-line decision summary live there; the reasoning lives here.
