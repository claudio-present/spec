# Traceability

Permanent, immutable log of everything that happened around a `Closed` [Open Question](./open-questions.md) — not just the final decision. Meeting notes, rejected alternatives and why they were rejected, research/data gathered along the way, the discussion itself: it all lives here. Keeps `open-questions.md` and `requirements/` clean — only the final requirement (in EARS) and a one-line decision summary live there; the noise lives here.
